The problem

Permitting is a black box. We built the map.

Every county runs its own portal. Every inspector has their own interpretation of the code. A HVAC subcontractor working across three Texas metros is essentially dealing with three different governments — with no playbook for any of them. The knowledge lives in one person's head, and the day she leaves, your timelines collapse.

📋
Zero standardization across jurisdictions
Form requirements, inspection sequences, fee schedules — all different. There is no federal template. Every municipality is its own puzzle.
🧠
Institutional knowledge that walks out the door
Most subs pay a full-time permit expediter $60–70K/year just to know which inspector needs a wet signature. That's an expensive single point of failure.
⏱️
Rejections cost more than the permit
A rejected application adds 5–10 days and idles a crew. On a $2M job, that's real money — and it happens on the exact forms your team has submitted before.

The founder
Dhruv Davda
Founder & CEO, Stradix

Stradix wasn't built from a whiteboard. It was built from watching a permit expediter become the single most important person in a contracting operation — and realizing that was a catastrophic design flaw.

Dhruv spent years inside the operational infrastructure of B2B distribution, watching the gap between what skilled operators knew and what structured systems could capture. He became obsessed with that asymmetry: the tacit knowledge that runs multi-million dollar operations, locked inside one person's head, invisible to any software.

Stradix is his answer to that problem — built first for the industry where the information gap costs the most: specialty contracting, where a single permit delay on a $2M job idles an entire crew.
